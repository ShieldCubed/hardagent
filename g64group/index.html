Introduction

G64 Group was founded in 2012. It is a privately held group controlled by Manuprasad, Vinay Krishnamurthy, their family members, and close associates. The group is headquartered in Mysore, India.

The house is a coordinating parent rather than a listed conglomerate. Member companies keep their own brands, sites, and operating mandates. G64 Group provides common control, long-horizon ownership, and a single point of introduction for counterparties who need to understand who stands behind the operating names.

Work across the group sits in three adjacent lines: frontier compute and research (Aumnium; Hardagent), digital-market infrastructure (StoxSwap), and specialist financial platforms (Softfino Bangk). The common thread is patient private ownership — not a public float, and not a marketing consortium assembled for a single raise.

Control

Control of G64 Group rests with Manuprasad, Vinay Krishnamurthy, family members, and close associates. There is no public listing of the parent and no implied offer of securities on this page.

Inquiries intended for the parent — as distinct from an operating company — may be routed through the relevant member site. Technical materials at Hardagent and Aumnium remain gated where those companies already require an NDA.
